Five skills

The science of leading under pressure, measured.

Your NLI scores tell you how strong each of these neuroadaptive skills are and where you can build greater strength and more healthy automatic responses.

01

Attune to disruption

Become keenly aware of the potential disruptions and pressures of everyday work and life. Train the prefrontal cortex to override emotional reaction and respond with intention rather than impulse.

02

Decode thought patterns

Understand and change the thinking patterns and biases that hinder effective problem-solving and decision-making. Recognize cognitive distortions, confirmation bias, and threat-response loops that cloud judgment under pressure.

03

Align identity and intention

Clarify who you are as a leader and what you stand for. When identity and intention are aligned, decisions become faster, communication becomes clearer, and your team can trust your behavior even when stakes are highest.

04

Program adaptivity

Rewire intentional and aligned behaviors to create automatic, healthy thinking and action. Build the neural pathways that make adaptive responses your default, not your exception, under sustained pressure.

05

Transfer to culture

Model neuroadaptive thinking and behavior to drive a neuroadaptive culture, making it a cultural competitive advantage. Teams that adapt faster, recover quicker, and perform better under pressure start with leaders who transfer these skills deliberately.
